Pi Streamer Remote

The base URL of your pi-streamer daemon (the same address the web UI is served from) — no trailing path. Uses POST /api/play/POST /api/queue once saved.

Don't know the address? Discovery tries common hostnames (works out of the box on a stock Raspberry Pi OS install). If your Pi has a custom hostname or mDNS doesn't resolve on your network, enter the address above manually instead.
